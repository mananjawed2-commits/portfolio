Über mich

About Me

I'm a motivated web developer from Pakistan with about 2–3 years of practical, hands-on experience building real websites for real clients — not just tutorials or personal exercises. Over that time I've worked across responsive website development, landing pages, e-commerce stores, UI/UX and graphic design, on-page SEO, and more recently AI-assisted development workflows.

Alongside development, I also work as a graphic designer — I'm comfortable in Figma for UI design and prototyping, and use it as part of my regular workflow for landing pages, website graphics and visual design work.

I'm currently preparing to move to Germany to complete a structured IT Ausbildung. My goal is to combine the practical skills I've already built with the discipline, mentorship and depth of a formal German training program, so I can grow into a reliable, well-rounded IT professional.

I stay curious about new tools and technologies, and I'm realistic about how much I still have to learn — which is exactly why an Ausbildung, rather than continuing to work alone, is the right next step for me.
